About Me

From IT graduate to data-driven operations specialist.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology and currently work as a Database & Operations Administrator at a telecommunications service cooperative, where I build internal tracking systems in Google Sheets and Apps Script so leadership has accurate visibility into membership data and key business metrics.

Along the way I’ve automated manual work such as receipt generation, handled high-volume customer operations with strict SOP adherence, and ensured data integrity as a Quality Assurance Specialist in content moderation. I’m also experienced in mentoring teams as a Workflow Trainer.

I’m now channeling that foundation in databases, automation and reporting into a Data Analyst career. I also use AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini to enhance my workflow and optimize my workload.

Education: BS Information Technology, ACLC Gapan City, Nueva Ecija (2012–2020). Coursework: Database Management, Computer Science, Data Entry, Systems Administration.
